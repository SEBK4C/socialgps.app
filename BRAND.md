## 5. THE EMOTIONAL RECEIPT (hero feature — deserves dedicated copy)

### What it is
The Emotional Receipt is the primary output of every Social GPS analysis. It's a timestamped visual and textual artifact that synthesises acoustic analysis, natural language processing, and communication psychology into a unified conversation debrief.

### What it contains
- **Emotion Map Visualisation:** A flower/crystal graphic using the Atlas of Emotions colour palette (Red = Anger, Purple = Fear, Blue = Sadness, Green = Disgust, Yellow = Enjoyment) with intensity driven by valence-arousal-dominance scoring.
- **Emotional Timeline:** A continuous emotional trajectory showing how the conversation's emotional temperature shifted moment by moment.
- **Self-Reflection Summary:** Focused exclusively on the user's own behaviour — Four Horsemen detection, persuasion pattern analysis, tactical empathy opportunities.
- **NVC Mirror:** The conversation's core emotional dynamics restated in observation-feeling-need-request format (Rosenberg).
- **Actionable Suggestions:** Framework-specific recommendations — repair attempts, calibrated questions, reframing options.
- **Vocal Power Assessment:** Pace, pause, pitch, and volume scoring (for public-facing use cases).
- **Destructive Pattern Alert:** Flagged Four Horsemen behaviours with severity scoring and repair suggestions.

### Copy angles for the Emotional Receipt
- "Your conversation, decoded."
- "Every conversation leaves an emotional fingerprint. Now you can see yours."
- "The debrief your therapist would give you — instantly, after every conversation."
- "A visual map of what really happened — not what you think happened."

## 9. ETHICAL POSITIONING (critical for copy — weave throughout)

### The self-focused design principle
Social GPS analyses what *you* could do better — never what the other person did wrong. This is the most important design decision in the product and should be reflected in every piece of copy. It's what makes the product:
- **Constructive** rather than accusatory
- **Empowering** rather than weaponisable
- **Ethically defensible** in an era of AI surveillance concerns
- **Regulatorily safe** under the EU AI Act (which bans workplace emotion surveillance but permits voluntary self-coaching tools)

### Privacy and consent
- Audio uploads require user attestation of consent from all parties
- No social credit scoring, gamification, or competitive ranking
- Cialdini analysis framed as educational awareness, not manipulation coaching
- Data is the user's — they control it, they delete it

### Copy to reinforce ethics
- "We analyse *your* side of the conversation — because real growth starts with self-awareness."
- "Social GPS will never be a surveillance tool. That's not a policy — it's the architecture."
- "Informed by Rosalind Picard's foundational work on ethical affective computing."

## 12. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (for website FAQ section)

**What is Social GPS?**
Social GPS is a conversational emotional intelligence platform. You upload a recording of any conversation, and we produce an Emotional Receipt — a visual and textual debrief showing the emotional dynamics, destructive patterns, and actionable coaching insights from that interaction.

**How is this different from meeting transcription tools?**
Transcription tools tell you *what* was said. We tell you *how* it felt — the emotional undercurrents, the trust dynamics, the missed opportunities for connection. The words are only half the story.

**What kind of conversations can I analyse?**
Any audio recording between 1–8 speakers, up to 120 minutes. Work meetings, podcast interviews, political speeches, personal conversations — if you can record it (with consent), we can analyse it.

**Is this therapy?**
No. Social GPS is an educational self-improvement tool, not a clinical instrument. We provide communication coaching grounded in published research, not psychological diagnosis or treatment.

**What about privacy?**
You must have consent from all parties before uploading a recording. We display consent reminders before every upload. Your data is yours — you control it, and you can delete it anytime.

**Why does it only focus on my behaviour?**
By design. Research shows that the most effective path to better communication starts with self-awareness, not partner blame. This also prevents the tool from being used as surveillance or as a weapon in arguments.

**What frameworks does Social GPS use?**
We integrate six research domains: Ekman's Atlas of Emotions, Gottman's Four Horsemen, Rosenberg's Nonviolent Communication, Cialdini's persuasion principles, Voss's tactical empathy, and computational paralinguistics (Schuller). Every insight traces back to published, peer-reviewed research.
